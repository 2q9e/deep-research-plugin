# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 13, 2026**

Deep Research Engine is a set of instructions and supporting files. It does not operate a server, use analytics or advertising, create accounts, or independently collect, transmit, or retain information.

## Information processed by your host

When you use the skill, your AI host processes the prompt and any materials you choose to provide. If you use the host's search, browser, or agent tools, those tools may send relevant queries or content to the services selected by that host. Information in public sources may also include personal data. Deep Research Engine does not control or retain those exchanges; the host and tool providers' privacy policies and settings govern their handling and retention.

The skill asks for no account credentials and does not require personal information. Avoid including sensitive personal data or secrets in research requests unless you have a lawful basis and the host and tools are appropriate for that data.

## Data recipients, retention, and controls

This package has no independent data recipients or storage, so it has no plugin-controlled retention period. The AI host and any retrieval providers you choose receive the information needed to answer your request and determine their own retention under their terms and settings. Use the host's privacy controls to manage or delete conversation data.

## Contact

For questions about this package's privacy behavior, contact the maintainer through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/2q9e/deep-research-plugin/issues).
